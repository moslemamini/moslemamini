## About me

I work where **machine-learning research meets real systems**, with a focus on **LLM/RAG systems, information retrieval, and adaptive time-series forecasting**.

- 🔬 Building reproducible experiments, data/model pipelines, and careful evaluation workflows.
- 🧠 Exploring semantic retrieval, embeddings, and retrieval-augmented generation.
- 📈 Developing adaptive approaches to financial time-series forecasting.
- 👁️ Experimenting with deep learning, computer vision, speech, and sequence modeling.
- 🎓 Interested in **research collaborations and PhD opportunities in AI and Machine Learning**.

> Clear questions. Reproducible experiments. Honest evaluation. Useful systems.
